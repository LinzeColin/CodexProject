Executive Verdict

NAB is IR-referenced, not yet IR-compliant.

The Annual Report contains strong strategy, governance, performance, sustainability and assurance ingredients. The gap is that those ingredients are not yet assembled into a designated, board-owned integrated report.

Stakeholders & Materiality

Sustainability materiality is good; integrated materiality is broader.

NAB's four-stage sustainability materiality process is credible. An integrated report should also capture enterprise value drivers: trust, conduct, cyber/AI, regulatory licence and financial capital resilience.

Map stakeholder needs to material matters
Classify by time horizon and affected capital
Show NAB's response, KPI and value consequence

Conciseness, Reliability & Comparability

Make assurance visible as trust architecture.

NAB has EY audit and selected limited assurance over non-financial metrics. The gap is not assurance existence; it is that assurance level is not mapped to material value drivers inside the value story.

Audited: financial report and remuneration report
Limited assured: selected non-financial metrics and RAP metrics
Needed: metric-level confidence labels
